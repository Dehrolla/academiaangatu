Foundations
Colors

Dark green, neon green and orange make up the brand. Warm cream and sand neutrals are the backgrounds, and white is used for cards. Orange is Angatu’s own addition: the template had none, and the live site uses it for every main call to action.

Typography

Spacing & layout

Spacing follows an 8-point rhythm. Sections have 120px of vertical padding (100px on tablet). Content sits in a 1400px container with a 24px gutter. The navbar is fixed and 72px tall.

Preciso de receita médica?

Sim. Ajudamos você a encontrar um médico prescritor parceiro.

A Angatu tem fins lucrativos?

Não. A Angatu é uma associação sem fins lucrativos.
